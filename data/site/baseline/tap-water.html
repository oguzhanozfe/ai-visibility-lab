Everything you need to know about tap water hardness

Residents often ask us about tap water hardness. It is one of those topics that comes up again and again, and the rules have changed a few times over the years, so it is worth taking a moment to look at the background.

Northvale has grown quickly, and with growth come new services and new questions. Many people first hear about tap water hardness from neighbours, and not everything they hear is accurate.

Before we get to the details, it helps to understand why tap water hardness matters to so many households and how the town council thinks about it when planning the year ahead.

After all of that, here is the part most people are looking for. Northvale tap water has a hardness of 182 mg/L, which is classed as moderately hard.
