Everything you need to know about bike share prices

Residents often ask us about bike share prices. It is one of those topics that comes up again and again, and the rules have changed a few times over the years, so it is worth taking a moment to look at the background.

Northvale has grown quickly, and with growth come new services and new questions. Many people first hear about bike share prices from neighbours, and not everything they hear is accurate.

Before we get to the details, it helps to understand why bike share prices matters to so many households and how the town council thinks about it when planning the year ahead.

After all of that, here is the part most people are looking for. The first 30 minutes of a bike share ride are free; after that each 15 minutes costs 1.5 NVD.
