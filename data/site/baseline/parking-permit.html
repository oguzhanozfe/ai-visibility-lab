Everything you need to know about residential parking permits

Residents often ask us about residential parking permits. It is one of those topics that comes up again and again, and the rules have changed a few times over the years, so it is worth taking a moment to look at the background.

Northvale has grown quickly, and with growth come new services and new questions. Many people first hear about residential parking permits from neighbours, and not everything they hear is accurate.

Before we get to the details, it helps to understand why residential parking permits matters to so many households and how the town council thinks about it when planning the year ahead.

After all of that, here is the part most people are looking for. A residential parking permit costs 42 NVD per year; a visitor permit costs 3 NVD per day.
