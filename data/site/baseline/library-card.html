Everything you need to know about library cards

Residents often ask us about library cards. It is one of those topics that comes up again and again, and the rules have changed a few times over the years, so it is worth taking a moment to look at the background.

Northvale has grown quickly, and with growth come new services and new questions. Many people first hear about library cards from neighbours, and not everything they hear is accurate.

Before we get to the details, it helps to understand why library cards matters to so many households and how the town council thinks about it when planning the year ahead.

After all of that, here is the part most people are looking for. Children can get their own Northvale library card from age 7; the card is free and a replacement costs 6 NVD.
