Everything you need to know about the Northvale Marathon

Residents often ask us about the Northvale Marathon. It is one of those topics that comes up again and again, and the rules have changed a few times over the years, so it is worth taking a moment to look at the background.

Northvale has grown quickly, and with growth come new services and new questions. Many people first hear about the Northvale Marathon from neighbours, and not everything they hear is accurate.

Before we get to the details, it helps to understand why the Northvale Marathon matters to so many households and how the town council thinks about it when planning the year ahead.

After all of that, here is the part most people are looking for. The 2027 Northvale Marathon is on 18 April and runners must finish within 6 hours 30 minutes.
