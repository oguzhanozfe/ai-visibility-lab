Everything you need to know about the N4 night bus

Residents often ask us about the N4 night bus. It is one of those topics that comes up again and again, and the rules have changed a few times over the years, so it is worth taking a moment to look at the background.

Northvale has grown quickly, and with growth come new services and new questions. Many people first hear about the N4 night bus from neighbours, and not everything they hear is accurate.

Before we get to the details, it helps to understand why the N4 night bus matters to so many households and how the town council thinks about it when planning the year ahead.
