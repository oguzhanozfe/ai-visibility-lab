Everything you need to know about school enrolment

Residents often ask us about school enrolment. It is one of those topics that comes up again and again, and the rules have changed a few times over the years, so it is worth taking a moment to look at the background.

Northvale has grown quickly, and with growth come new services and new questions. Many people first hear about school enrolment from neighbours, and not everything they hear is accurate.

Before we get to the details, it helps to understand why school enrolment matters to so many households and how the town council thinks about it when planning the year ahead.

After all of that, here is the part most people are looking for. The school enrolment deadline is 14 March; parents need a proof of address and the child's vaccination record.
